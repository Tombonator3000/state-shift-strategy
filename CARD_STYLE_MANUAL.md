# 🎴 Card Style Manual: Tabloid Conspiracy Comedy Edition

## 📰 **Core Philosophy**

This game satirizes conspiracy culture and government overreach through **darkly comic tabloid absurdism**. Every card should feel like it came from:
- A paranoid late-night AM radio show (Truth faction)
- A suspiciously calm government press release (Government faction)

**Golden Rule**: If you can't imagine it as a *Weekly World News* headline or a redacted CIA memo, rewrite it.

---

## 🏛️ **GOVERNMENT FACTION - "Sinister Bureaucrat Comedy"**

### Tone Target
Think: *1984* meets *The Office*. Kafka with a paycheck. Evil, but filed in triplicate.

### Naming Patterns

#### ✅ **GOOD Government Card Names:**
| Pattern | Example | Why It Works |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Euphemism + Implied Threat** | "Voluntary Silence Zones" | "Voluntary" doing a LOT of work here |
| **Mundane Object + Sinister Context** | "Cigarette Whisperer" | Smoking = spy thriller vibe |
| **Over-Classification Joke** | "Triple-Classified Briefing Room" | Absurdist escalation |
| **Bureaucratic Passive Voice** | "Declassification (Pending: 2177)" | Institutional immortality humor |
| **Operation [Silly Code Name]** | "Operation Mockingbird II: Electric Boogaloo" | Real op + sequel joke |
| **Specific Conspiracy Location** | "Denver Airport Bunker" | Real conspiracy theory! |

#### ❌ **BAD Government Card Names:**
| Anti-Pattern | Example | Why It Fails |
|-------------|---------|-------------|
| Too Generic | "Security Protocol" | Could be from any game |
| Too Honest | "Narrative Control" | Where's the euphemism? |
| Corporate Jargon | "Stakeholder Alignment Initiative" | Not funny, just boring |
| Missing Punchline | "Information Management" | So what? |

### Flavor Text Formula

**[Dry Observation] + [Dark Implication]**

✅ **Examples:**
- "Your transparency request is aging like fine wine." *(Patient menace)*
- "Gag orders pair well with morning coffee." *(Casual evil)*
- "Democracy is great. Backups are greater." *(Sinister pragmatism)*
- "Officially: meteorology. Unofficially: [REDACTED]." *(Wink-wink denial)*

❌ **Avoid:**
- "Control the story, control reality." *(Too dramatic, not funny)*
- "Power consolidates itself." *(Philosophy, not satire)*
- "The machine runs on influence." *(Vague, no punchline)*

### Character Archetypes (Government)
- **Agent Smitherson**: The one with a conscience (barely)
- **Deputy Director Karen Walsh**: Career bureaucrat, dead inside
- **General Marcus Thompson**: Military euphemism generator
- **Classification Czar Donald Pierce**: Over-classifies his lunch order

## 🔍 **TRUTH FACTION - "Paranoid Tabloid Maximalism"**

### Tone Target
Think: *X-Files* opening credits meets your uncle's Facebook feed. True believers with photocopiers. Amateur detectives with too much free time.

### Naming Patterns

#### ✅ **GOOD Truth Card Names:**
| Pattern | Example | Why It Works |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Character + Absurd Action** | "Elvis Works the Night Shift" | Specific + weird detail |
| **DIY Conspiracy Setup** | "Garage CSI: Paranormal Unit" | Amateur hour energy |
| **Location + Conspiracy Twist** | "Neighborhood Watch (For UFOs)" | Mundane → paranoid |
| **Tabloid Headline Energy** | "Blurry Mothman Dashcam" | Could be real Weekly World News |
| **Resistance LARPing** | "FOIA Revenge Squad" | Taking it VERY seriously |
| **Kooky Products** | "Industrial Tinfoil Distributor" | Conspiracy capitalism |

#### ❌ **BAD Truth Card Names:**
| Anti-Pattern | Example | Why It Fails |
|-------------|---------|-------------|
| Generic Activism | "Grassroots Movement" | Too earnest, not weird |
| Straight Journalism | "Open-Records Taskforce" | Sounds like real NGO |
| Missing The Weird | "Community Network" | Where's the conspiracy? |
| Too Competent | "Strategic Intelligence Unit" | These are amateurs! |

### Flavor Text Formula

**[Specific Weird Detail] + [Deadpan Delivery]**

✅ **Examples:**
- "Screams in AP style." *(Perfect absurdist precision)*
- "Wingspan: entire windshield." *(Specific + matter-of-fact)*
- "First question: favorite ice cream?" *(Kid innocence + alien interview)*
- "Vows exchanged, lies annulled." *(Elvis chapel poetry)*
- "Single-origin skepticism." *(Coffee culture + conspiracy)*

❌ **Avoid:**
- "Nothing unites truth seekers like oppression." *(Too earnest)*
- "Truth begets more truth." *(Vague philosophy)*
- "Every territory amplifies the signal." *(Generic)*

### Character Archetypes (Truth)
- **Pastor Rex**: Apocalypse preacher + product placement
- **Elvis**: Alive, working odd jobs, mysteriously helpful
- **Bat Boy**: Former tabloid mascot, now political activist
- **Florida Man**: Chaos agent with citrus-scented flares
- **Maria Chen**: Ham radio detective, subway office

## 🎨 **WRITING RECIPES**

### Recipe 1: "Mundane Location + Conspiracy Twist"
**Formula**: `[Normal Place] + [Paranoid Purpose]`

Examples:
- "Neighborhood Watch (For UFOs)"
- "PTA Meeting (Disclosure Caucus)"
- "Book Club (Classified Documents Division)"
- "Bingo Night (Cryptid Sightings Exchange)"

### Recipe 2: "Bureaucratic Euphemism + Dark Reality"
**Formula**: `[Friendly Word] + [Sinister Context]`

Examples:
- "Voluntary Silence Zones"
- "Friendly National Security Letter"
- "Helpful Memory Workshop"
- "Routine Asset Seizure"

### Recipe 3: "Character Does Absurd Thing"
**Formula**: `[Named Character] + [Unexpected Action] + [Conspiracy Context]`

Examples:
- "Elvis Edits the Front Page"
- "Bat Boy Launches a Zine"
- "Pastor Rex Predicts Disclosure"
- "Florida Man Finds Government Device"

### Recipe 4: "Over-Specific Detail Comedy"
**Formula**: `[Thing] + [Hilariously Precise Number/Detail]`

Examples:
- "No windows, two clocks, three alarms."
- "Declassification (Pending: 2177)"
- "Crates labeled 'Weather Balloon' (wink)."
- "47-page analysis conclusively demonstrates..."

## 📏 **TECHNICAL SPECS**

### Card Name Length
- **Ideal**: 3-6 words
- **Max**: 8 words
- **Min**: 2 words (rare, must be punchy)

### Flavor Text Length
- **Ideal**: 6-12 words (punchline speed)
- **Max**: 20 words (only for complex jokes)
- **Min**: 4 words (only if devastatingly funny)

### Flavor Text Structure
```
[Setup] + [Punchline]
```
OR
```
[Dry Observation] + [Dark Implication]
```
OR  
```
[Specific Detail] + [Deadpan Closer]
```

## 🚫 **FORBIDDEN WORDS LIST**

Words that make cards too serious/generic:

### Government Faction
❌ "Protocol" (unless twisted: "Wink-Wink Protocol")  
❌ "Initiative" (unless absurd: "Citizen Happiness Initiative")  
❌ "Framework" (bureaucrat bingo word)  
❌ "Stakeholder" (kill on sight)  
❌ "Synergy" (absolutely not)  

### Truth Faction
❌ "Movement" (unless specific: "Bigfoot Voter Registration")  
❌ "Network" (unless twisted: "Ham Radio Quilting Network")  
❌ "Coalition" (too organized for these weirdos)  
❌ "Strategic" (they're amateurs!)  
❌ "Professional" (same)

## 🎯 **QUALITY CHECKLIST**

Before finalizing any card, ask:

- [ ] Would this make someone laugh OR feel paranoid?
- [ ] Is there a specific detail (not vague concepts)?
- [ ] Does it reference real conspiracy culture?
- [ ] Would it work as a tabloid headline or memo?
- [ ] Is there a "punchline" or dark joke?
- [ ] Could a character from the lore appear here?
- [ ] Does it avoid generic activism/corporate speak?
- [ ] Is the tone consistent with faction identity?

**If 6/8 = YES → Ship it**  
**If 4/8 = YES → Needs work**  
**If <4/8 = YES → Rewrite from scratch**

## 🔄 **REVISION PROCESS**

### Step 1: Identify The Boring
Is the card name generic? Could swap "truth" and "government" and it'd still make sense?

### Step 2: Find The Weird
What's the most absurd TRUE thing about this concept?  
(Government: real euphemisms, Truth: real conspiracy theories)

### Step 3: Add Specificity
Replace vague words with precise details. "Network" → "Ham Radio Quilting Circle"

### Step 4: Punch Up Flavor
Last sentence should land like a punchline or ominous whisper.

## 💬 **VOICE GUIDE**

### Government Voice
- **Passive constructions**: "Has been determined", "Will be addressed"
- **Vague timeframes**: "Shortly", "Eventually", "In due course"
- **Euphemisms**: "Voluntary", "Routine", "Friendly"
- **Redactions**: Use [REDACTED] for comedy
- **Numbered items**: "Protocol 7", "Annex B", "Subsection 4"

### Truth Voice
- **Breathless urgency**: "BREAKING", "EXCLUSIVE", "LEAKED"
- **Specific details**: "3:15 AM", "47 witnesses", "Exactly three times"
- **Question marks**: End with suspicion
- **ALL CAPS**: For emphasis (sparingly)
- **Brand names**: "Doomsday Beans™", "TruthMeter™"
