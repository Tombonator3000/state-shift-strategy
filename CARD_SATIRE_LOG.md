## 🎨 **STYLE PATTERNS IDENTIFIED**

### ✅ **WINNERS** (Keep This Energy)
- **Mundane + Conspiracy**: "Weather Balloon Maneuvers", "Garage CSI"
- **Euphemism + Dark Joke**: "Voluntary Silence Zones", "Friendly NSL"
- **Specific References**: "Denver Airport Bunker" (real conspiracy!)
- **Character Names**: "Pastor Rex", "Elvis", "Bat Boy", "Florida Man"
- **Absurd Precision**: "Three alarms", "Exactly 47 pages", "2177"

### ❌ **LOSERS** (Avoid/Replace)
- Generic activism words: "Movement", "Rally", "Network" (unless twisted)
- Straight corporate: "Protocol", "Initiative", "Framework"
- Too on-the-nose: "Narrative Control", "Total Information Awareness"
- Missing punchline: "Control the story" (so what?)
